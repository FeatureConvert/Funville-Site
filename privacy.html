Funville Privacy Policy

Funville: Idle Adventure Park · Last updated October 2, 2026

Short version: Funville has no sign-up, no ads and no tracking, and it doesn't collect personal information. If you say yes when the game asks, it sends us anonymous error and slowdown reports so we can fix problems. If you choose to join the rankings, your park's name and scores are shown to other players. You can change either any time.

What we collect

No personal information. Funville has no sign-up, no ad networks, no analytics and no tracking. We don't know who plays, and we never see your saved park. The rankings are optional and use only a park name you choose (see below).

Anonymous error and slowdown reports

On your second visit the game asks "Help us fix bugs?". Only if you tap "Send reports" does it send us a short report when something goes wrong inside the game, and, at most once a day, a summary when it has been running slowly.

A report contains the game's version (and its engine's), your device's model (for example "iPhone17,1") and its iOS version, the game's graphics setting, how long the game had been open, and what went wrong inside the game. A slowdown summary has frame-rate figures instead, which screen was showing and how busy it was (for example how many guests were on it), and whether battery saver was on. Reports have no name, no account, no device or advertising identifier and nothing from your saved park, so they can't be linked to you.

Reports are stored with Cloudflare, which runs our server, and are deleted after 90 days. Your IP address is used only to limit how many reports can arrive in a minute and is never stored, and we keep no request logs. You can turn reports on or off at any time: More, then Settings, then "Send error reports".

Event dates and game settings

When the game starts, and when you come back to it after a few hours, it downloads a small settings file from our server (for example the dates of seasonal events), so we can adjust them without an app update. That request only says which version of the game it is and that it's the iOS version. Nothing about you is sent or kept.

Our server runs on Cloudflare, which handles the connection to deliver it (see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy).

Rankings (only if you join)

World Rankings and the Weekly Cup's standings are optional. To join, you choose a name for your park. Please don't use your real name or anything personal in it. Everyone who plays can see your park's name, its place, its all-time earnings and Owner Level, and its Weekly Cup score.

When you join, our server gives your park a random player number, which is shown with the rankings, and a secret code that stays with you: your device keeps it, and so does your park's iCloud copy if you use Game Center, so your other devices can use it too. The server stores a scrambled version of it, not the code itself. The server keeps the park name and whether it has been checked; your all-time earnings and Owner Level; your Weekly Cup score for each of the last 12 weeks; when you joined, last renamed and last sent a score; and any reports of park names you made or received. It doesn't keep your real name, an account, a Game Center or device identifier, or your IP address.

Park names are checked before they're shown: by our own word list, and by an automatic safety check that runs on Cloudflare (Workers AI). Players can report a name. A reported name may be hidden until we look at it, and we may hide or block names that break the rules.

You can rename your park once a day. To leave, open World Rankings and tap "Leave the rankings": everything above is deleted from our server straight away. If a park sends no scores for 400 days, it is deleted too, and Weekly Cup scores older than 12 weeks are deleted every day.

Your saved park

Your park is saved on your device. If you're signed in to Game Center, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud (Game Center saved games) so you can pick up on a new device. That copy is stored by Apple in your iCloud account; we can't see or access it.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, achievements and leaderboard scores are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Funville only sends Game Center your in-game progress (for example your Owner Level and Weekly Cup score).

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The game only learns which item you bought so it can give it to you.

Notifications

If you allow them, Funville schedules reminders on your device (for example when your park's away earnings are full). They're created on the device itself; nothing is sent to us.

Children

Funville is made for everyone, including families. We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children. Error and slowdown reports are anonymous and contain nothing about the player. The rankings are optional; a park name should never include a real name, and names are checked before they're shown.

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
